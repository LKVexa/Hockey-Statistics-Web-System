What runs here. The engine of RINK-VT, unchanged: the command language, 64-byte frames, the event-sourced game actor, xG, RAPM, GSAx and GAR, the roster importer and the HK001 port. It runs in this window, which is the renderer; nothing here is Node.js.
Where the league is. The Photon host, a native program on this computer (no Python, no Node.js), keeps the registry and a hash-chained ledger per game. Each line is on its disk before its result appears; store verify re-reads all of it. Your roster file is read in this window.
Data. The four demonstration clubs and anything simulated are fictional. HK001 is a deterministic synthetic research laboratory. It gives no forecasts, odds, or roster, medical, scouting or officiating advice.
